# Thebus — referee's notes

The scenario's own description is what the players see in the picker, so it
says only that a yacht is calling for help. Everything the referee needs is
here. Source: *Treasures of Sindal*, Pirates of Drinax book 1 p. 128, with the
PRC ship list on p. 141.

## The trap

Lyonesse II is a Proactive Recycling Company Q-ship: bait for any pirate who
jumps into Thebus. Behind the moonlet sit the Star Ray interceptors
**Invigilator** — President Yang aboard — and **Death's Actuary**, and the
Vulture-class salvage hauler **Cicada**. They spring it once the Travellers
are committed.

The ships carry what the fleet list gives them rather than what our designs
carry as standard: two *double* beam turrets on each Star Ray, a double pulse
turret and a mixed sandcaster/missile turret on the Vulture, and a pop-up
triple pulse turret on the yacht.

## The opening position

- HMS Executor starts 4,000 km from the yacht; the three ambushers are 8,000
  to 9,000 km behind the moonlet. That is Medium range — inside the
  interceptors' beam lasers — so the trap bites the round it springs.
- Move everyone to wherever the approach actually ended. The position in the
  file is only a sensible default.
- Lyonesse II is deliberately unaligned: a team colour on the board would give
  the ruse away. Put her on the PRC team at the moment she drops the act.
- Awareness starts one-way. Lyonesse II holds the Travellers and talks to the
  ships behind the moonlet, who hold only her, so she hands the Travellers
  over in round one — a point of Bandwidth at each end, nothing on her
  Computer/5. Jamming her comms is therefore worth something.
- The lurkers are dark, and have to be detected or will announce themselves by
  shooting.

## Two things from the text

- The yacht's jump drive is shot and irreparable, so her jump rating is 0, and
  the PRC will spend her as a fireship if the battle turns: nuclear missiles in
  the cargo bay, 6D to every ship at Close range.
- Death's Actuary is under orders to protect Yang's ship at all costs. The PRC
  are not bloodthirsty and will listen to an offer.
