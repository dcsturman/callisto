Something is out there you have not found: a stealthed hull is invisible until a sensor roll beats its coating, and it will choose when to strike. Find it before it finds the moment. Run active sensors on the ship with the best sensors and operator, and share its picture so the rest can stay quiet and still shoot; give it the captain's boost on the search. Once you hold a stealthed ship, lock it, keep the range from opening by a band, which is when it slips contact, and put every gun on it while you can see it. Firing gives a stealthed ship away: where it fired from is where to look.
