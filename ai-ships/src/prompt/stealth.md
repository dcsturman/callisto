Its coating only helps while it stays `silent`: active sensors, transmitting and firing all add to the roll against it, and the exposure lines show by how much. So a stealthed ship stays silent, lets a team-mate that will be seen anyway do the searching and share the picture (a hand-off reaches it without it transmitting anything), and closes unseen to the band where its guns reach. Hiding is the means, not the aim: once she is in reach of a target the side is already hitting, she fires, every round it is worth it, because an unseen ship's shots are the ones the enemy cannot answer. She does not sit unseen in range and hold her fire. When she has been found, or the enemy closes on her, she opens the range by a band to shake the contact, goes silent again, and comes back. A ship holding no contact and no team-mate to search for it must either go active and be found, or accept that it is blind.
